HoodPacks protocol design and tokenomics.

Overview

HoodPacks is a closed-loop card pack economy on Robinhood Chain. Collectors spend $HPACK to open packs, pull cards across five rarity tiers, and trade what they pull for $HPACK, USDC, or directly with other collectors. No lottery, no draws, no off-chain custody.

Token: $HPACK

$HPACK is the native token of the HoodPacks economy. Every pack opened burns a share of the spend, so supply contracts as the collector base grows.

The Hood Loop

The economy runs as a closed loop. $HPACK enters through pack opens, cards mint to the collector, and value returns through the trade market. A share of every trade fee burns, which keeps the loop deflationary instead of extractive.

  1. Open — Pay the pack price in $HPACK. Every pull is provably fair and settled on Robinhood Chain.
  2. Collect — Cards land in your vault. Common through Legendary, every pull is yours to hold.
  3. Trade — Sell into $HPACK, USDC, or swap direct with another collector. 2.5% fee, partially burned.
